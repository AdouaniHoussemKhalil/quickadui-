# Core

`@quickadui/core` is the everyday visual vocabulary — buttons, cards,
badges, alerts, and the rest of what most screens are built from. Every
part here composes `@quickadui/primitives` underneath and ships real,
default Tailwind styling (unlike most of `primitives` itself).

## IconButton

An icon-only button — unlike `Button`, `aria-label` is a *required* prop:
there's no visible text for a screen reader to fall back on without one.

## Tooltip

`TooltipProvider` must wrap the app (or subtree) exactly once, high in the
tree — it's what makes a second tooltip skip the opening delay. It's
included in this example only because this page has no app-wide one of
its own.

<Example
  code={`<TooltipProvider>
  <Tooltip>
    <TooltipTrigger asChild>

</TooltipProvider>`}
